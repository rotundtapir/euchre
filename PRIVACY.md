# Privacy Policy — Euchre

**Effective: 2026-07-29**

Euchre is a free, open-source card game. This policy covers every distribution of the app.

## The short version

Playing offline — against bots, or the tutorial — makes **no network connections at all**, in every
build. Online multiplayer is opt-in: it connects only when you choose to play online, only to the
game server you point it at, and it needs no account. There are no analytics or trackers in any
build. The Google Play build additionally shows ads.

> **Changed in 0.2.0.** Earlier versions could not connect to anything: the FOSS build did not
> request the `INTERNET` permission at all. Adding online multiplayer required it. If you never open
> the online section, the app still makes no connections — but the permission is now present, and
> this policy no longer claims the app is incapable of networking.

## Offline play (all builds)

- No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no accounts, no network connections.
- Settings (animation speed, house rules, sound volume, tutorial progress) are stored only on your
  device.

## Online multiplayer (all builds, opt-in)

Online play connects to a game server over an encrypted WebSocket. The default server is the
project's own (`wss://euchre.29022617.xyz`); you can change it in Settings → Online, or
[run your own](https://github.com/rotundtapir/euchre) — the server is part of this repository and is
free software too.

**What leaves your device, and only while you are playing online:**

- The **display name you type** for that session. It is not linked to anything else and is not kept
  after the room ends.
- Your **moves** — the cards and bids you choose.
- **Canned emotes** from a fixed list. There is no free-text chat, so there is nothing to moderate
  and nothing you can accidentally say.
- An **anonymous session token**, generated by the server, which is what lets you reclaim your seat
  after a dropped connection. It identifies a seat, not a person, and is discarded when you leave.
- **Build details** — app version, platform (Android or web), distribution flavour, and the source
  commit — so the server can tell an outdated client to update and so cross-platform bugs can be
  diagnosed.

**What the server keeps:**

- Your **IP address**, in ordinary connection logs, used to rate-limit abuse and block hosts that
  attack the server. Logs rotate and are capped.
- While a game is in progress, a **snapshot of that game on disk**, so an in-flight match survives a
  server restart instead of being lost. It is deleted when the room ends.
- No accounts, no email addresses, no profiles, no analytics, and nothing that outlives the room you
  played in.

## Web version (rotundtapir.github.io/euchre)

- Settings are stored in your browser's local storage. Offline play makes no requests after the page
  loads; online play behaves as described above.
- The site is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may log requests (IP address, user agent) when the page
  and its assets load, per [GitHub's privacy
  statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Google Play build

Identical gameplay, plus advertising and an optional remove-ads purchase:

- **Google AdMob** shows a banner and an occasional interstitial. AdMob may collect device
  identifiers and ad-interaction data as described in
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
  [how Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- In regions requiring consent, Google's **User Messaging Platform** consent form is shown before
  any ad; you can revisit it via Settings → Privacy options.
- **Google Play Billing** processes the remove-ads purchase; we never see your payment details.
  After the purchase, ads stop being requested.
- The FOSS build contains none of the above: those SDKs are in a separate module that the FOSS build
  cannot reach, which CI verifies on every change.
- The app itself still has no analytics, accounts, or data collection of its own.
