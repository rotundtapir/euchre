Online multiplayer is here.

• Play with friends: share a 4-character code. Android and browser play together.
• Bots fill empty seats, and cover anyone who drops until they rejoin.
• A lost connection — or even a server restart — doesn't cost you the game.
• House rules work online too.

Offline play still makes no network connections, and online needs no account. You can run your own
server if you'd rather; it's open source like the rest.
